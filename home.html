In Type Soul, Soul Reapers (Shinigami) are one of the primary playable races relying on spiritual pressure and swordplay Players begin as Academy Students and advance by refining their combat skills, flash steps, and Zanpakuto mastery. A Soul Reaper's power spikes significantly upon unlocking Shikai, achieved by meditating and defeating their inner spirit to gain elemental powers. True mastery culminates in unlocking Bankai, granting immense area-of-effect damage, distinctive weapon forms, and devastating ultimate abilities. Balancing high-speed melee strikes, Kido spells, and versatile skill trees, Soul Reapers excel as versatile, high-mobility combatants in both competitive PvP and faction battles.

About
• Type Soul

In type soul, you first start out by creating your character. You have to pick between the four races: Soul reaper, Arrancars, Quincy or Fullbringers. Each race fits a different play style and you can learn more about races here. Each race has their different faction bases . which all have their unique bosses, unique loot and an array of missions. The progression of your character follows : select a build for your race, then, according to the build, level up your character and put in the correct stats. Once you are "Semi Grade 2" level, you are able to unlock your element. The next step of your progression is the hardest, once you are max level, you are able to obtain full mode. Your full mode enables you to change the tide of a battle with a single button. However, the quest to obtain it has caused many casuals to forfeit.

Frequently asked

12 frequently asked questions to clarify about the game

What is the character creation?

In type soul, you start the game with creating your character. You have to choose between 4 races. These  CANNOT  be changed later on so be really careful on what you pick.

What are elements?

Elements are secondary abilities that you use alongside your main skill tree abilities. These help bring your build to its full potential. You can unlock this after half of your progression.

What is a Full Mode?

Unlike normal elements, your full mode is not a passive. You have to activate it in order to use it. To unlock this mode, you require a vast amount of progression after you have maxed out your stats/ levels. Your full mode enhances your element.

What race to pick?

Each race has their own different playstyles. In order to find out what suits your playstyle, you can use  this  as a guide.

What is a skill tree?

There are 7 skill trees in the game. Each will enable you to different abilities and passives. Every skill tree is unique to different playstyles. You are able to go more than 1 skill tree, allowing for diverse builds.

What is a build?

A Build comprises of the stats you put into a skill tree along side your element. Each build suits a different playstyle. You can use this  to choose your build or maybe even create your own build.

What are factions?

A faction is the primary base your race uses. Each faction has unique features such as bosses, clothing items and drops. You are able to enter different faction bases if you enter their portal. This allows for "ganks". You can check out factions  here .

What is a gank?

A gank is when many players, 2 or more groups of players continuously attack each other. This is primarily seen during faction raids. Its best to avoid ganks as a new player as will lose money that you need to progress and you might lose loot.

What is a faction raid?

Every other hour, a raid will happen in every faction base. This allows the home faction to fight off opposing factions that are in their base. The winner will earn various loot items and supply caches will drop. These caches are avaliable to all, even the losing factions so be quick!

What is a cheese build?

A cheese build is basically when your build requires no skill to play or has a gimmick that makes it very strong and annoying to fight against. As a new player, its perfectly fine to play cheese. However, as you progress, its more respectable to play skillful builds.
